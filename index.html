Publications

Relationship Banking: The Borrower's Incentive Channel
Journal of Corporate Finance

We examine whether prior banking relationships affect borrowers' incentives to avoid default using data on 149,230 mortgage loans. We find that the presence of a banking relationship reduces borrower default risk, consistent with borrowers valuing the continuation of their relationship with the bank.
